Methodology, validation scope & data handling

Forward model: exact Burmister/Schiffman multilayer elastic solution (the theory class used by WESLEA/EVERCALC), verified against the closed-form Boussinesq half-space solution (<0.02% error). Backcalculation: bounded least-squares minimization of relative deflection error (EVERCALC's objective) in log-modulus space with multi-start.

Validated scope. Validation covers exactly four WSDOT SR-395 deflection basins from FHWA-HRT-16-011 Chapter 3 (MP207.85, MP208.00, MP208.50, MP209.00), compared against the EVERCALC results published in that chapter only. On three of the four sections the backcalculated moduli match EVERCALC within ~10% with equal or better basin RMS; the fourth (MP208.50, 119 mm HMA) is a thin-HMA case whose best fit sits at the HMA upper bound. No comparison against BAKFAA or any other program has been performed, and accuracy outside these four basins is not claimed.

Results at a bound. A modulus that lands on its E min / E max bound is flagged as needing engineering review: the optimum wanted to go beyond the bound, so treat the value as a limit, not a measurement. Use lab- or temperature-adjusted seeds, tighter bounds, or the Fix E option on that layer.

Data handling. The deflections and layer inputs you enter are sent to this site's server endpoint (/api/backcalc) for computation. The app does not store your inputs.
