Methodology & validation

Forward model: exact Burmister/Schiffman multilayer elastic solution (the theory class used by WESLEA/EVERCALC), verified against the closed-form Boussinesq half-space solution (<0.02% error).

Backcalculation: bounded least-squares minimization of relative deflection error (EVERCALC objective) in log-modulus space with multi-start.

Validated against the FHWA-HRT-16-011 Chapter 3 field example (WSDOT SR-395 basins): backcalculated moduli match EVERCALC within ~10% on 3 of 4 sections with equal or better basin fit (RMS); the 4th is a thin-HMA case with the known modulus trade-off flagged by FHWA guidance.
